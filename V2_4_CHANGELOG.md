# SPIKE v2.4 — Intelligence & Proactive Growth

- Added SPIKE Intelligence center with proactive business alerts.
- Added context-readiness view so missing business information is visible instead of guessed.
- Added experiment-memory surface showing what the owner has already tried and its recorded result.
- Connected alerts to relevant SPIKE areas (Money, Customers, Actions, Growth and SPIKE AI).

## Safety and trust

Alerts are derived from information the owner has recorded. They are not forecasts, guarantees, performance scores, or claims about hidden business data.
